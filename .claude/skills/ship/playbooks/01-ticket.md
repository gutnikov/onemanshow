# Ticket

An idea has arrived and there is nothing to track it. Make a ticket, put it in
`draft`, and reply in the thread with its number.

Do this **immediately**, before sharpening the idea. The ticket is where state
lives; until it exists, the skill has nowhere to read from and a second
invocation cannot find this conversation.

## What goes in it

The idea in the person's own words, and a link back to this thread. Do not
improve the wording yet — that is `draft`, and the person may want the original
to compare against.

## The link is bidirectional

The thread URL goes on the ticket, and the ticket number goes in the thread.
Either end can then find the other, and no separate record is needed. Skipping
one half creates state that lives nowhere.

Then continue into `playbooks/02-draft.md` in the same invocation. A ticket
sitting in `draft` with nothing in it is not a useful place to stop.
